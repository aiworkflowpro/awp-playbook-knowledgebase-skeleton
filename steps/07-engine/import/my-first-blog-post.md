# 5 Things I Learned Building AI Workflows

I started automating my content pipeline three months ago. Here is what I wish someone told me on day one.

1. Start with one workflow, not five. I tried to automate everything at once and nothing worked.
2. The agent needs context. Without a knowledge base, it forgets everything between sessions.
3. Naming matters more than you think. Random filenames make search impossible after a month.
4. Let the agent check its own work. Add a verification step to every workflow.
5. Ship weekly. A workflow that runs every week beats a perfect one that never ships.

This could be a YouTube script or a newsletter issue.
