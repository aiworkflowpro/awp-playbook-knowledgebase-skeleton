# Weekly Video Script

## Trigger
When I say "new video on [topic]".

## Steps
1. Ask me what the video is about — one sentence.
2. Search the web for the latest news and tutorials on this topic.
3. Ask me three questions: my angle, the audience pain point, and the core argument.
4. Write a full voiceover script: hook (under 50 words), three sections, CTA at the end.
5. Apply the brand voice from brand/identity.md.

## Check
- Hook is under 50 words.
- Three sections, each starting with a one-line summary.
- CTA mentions subscribe and the next video.
- No jargon — plain English only.
- Filename follows the naming convention in specs/.
