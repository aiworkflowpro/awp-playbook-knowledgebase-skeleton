# Competitor Channels

> Who else teaches AI agents on YouTube — their format, size, and cadence, and where AI Workflow Pro is different. Snapshots accumulate by month, because subscriber counts and formats change.

## Scope
What this topic covers:
- Channels covering AI agents, AI workflows, and AI tools — format, size, publishing cadence
- What each channel does well and what it leaves uncovered
- Where AI Workflow Pro is different from them

What belongs here: channel snapshots with a date, format breakdowns, positioning notes.
What does not: the brand's own voice and positioning statements (→ `brand/identity.md`), audience definition (→ `brand/audience.md`), channel operations (→ `business/youtube/`).

## Search
How to find new material: YouTube search for "AI agents tutorial", "AI workflow automation", "Claude Code tutorial"; check the channels already tracked here for format changes. Write a dated snapshot into `materials/{YYYYMM}/` rather than editing an old one — the point is the change over time.
