# Standardized — AI editing workflow video

## What It Is
A YouTube tutorial on using AI to speed up video editing, focused on two high-value, easy steps: AI subtitles and AI clean audio.

## Key Points
- **AI subtitles** — one click produces clean, styled captions. Viewers watch with sound off; captions also help YouTube search.
- **AI clean audio** — one click removes background noise. No manual audio work needed.
- These two steps save the most time for the least effort — a good first move for anyone weak at editing.

## Why It Matters for Leo
Leo said editing is his weak spot. These two steps are the cheapest quality wins: subtitles improve accessibility and search, clean audio improves watchability. Both fit the "AI does the first pass" mindset.

## Key Takeaway
Start every edit with AI subtitles + AI clean audio. Watch the video for the exact tool steps.
