# Standardized — AI video tools 2026

## What It Is
A 2026 comparison of AI video editing tools. Core insight: there is no single winner — each tool is best for one specific job.

## Tool Map (best at one job each)
| Job | Tool |
|-----|------|
| Auto-clip long videos into shorts | Opus Clip |
| Text-based editing (edit by editing transcript) | Descript |
| Generative video (create new footage) | Veo 3.1, Runway Gen-4.5 |
| Dubbing / voice | ElevenLabs, HeyGen |
| AI avatars | Synthesia |
| Video understanding / search | Twelve Labs |

## Why It Matters for Leo
Leo's business needs: YouTube videos → short clips for X (Opus Clip class), fixing voiceover/audio (ElevenLabs class), and text-based editing (Descript class). Choosing by job, not by "best tool", matches his workflow thinking.

## Key Takeaway
Pick the tool per job. For AWP: auto-shorts (Opus Clip) and text-based editing (Descript) are the two most relevant first picks.
