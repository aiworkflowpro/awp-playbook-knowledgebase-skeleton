# Standardized — editing techniques for quality

## What It Is
An article listing editing techniques that raise video quality — the craft side of editing, complementary to the AI tools side.

## Key Techniques
- **Proxy editing** — work with low-resolution copies of high-resolution footage; edit fast, then switch back to full quality at export.
- **Organize media first** — folders for footage, graphics, audio, drafts. A tidy project edits faster and with fewer mistakes.
- **Plan before editing** — know the structure before cutting; choose clips that show important action.
- **Front-load value** — first 30 seconds tell viewers what they get (YouTube rewards retention).
- **Use chapters** — timestamps in the description help search and give viewers control.

## Why It Matters for Leo
Leo wants to raise overall production quality. These are the craft techniques that make videos feel professional — and they are all structured steps, which fits Leo's framework-driven thinking.

## Key Takeaway
Quality comes from: organized media + planned structure + proxy editing + front-loaded value. Apply these before touching fancy effects.
