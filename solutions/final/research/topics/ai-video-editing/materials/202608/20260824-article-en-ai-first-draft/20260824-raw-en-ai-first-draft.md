# Raw — AI first draft methodology

Source: https://pixflow.net/blog/efficient-post-production-video-editing-workflow
Type: article
Found: web search, 2026-08-24

Original excerpt:
> Treat AI as a first draft, not a final decision. Let AI handle the initial pass (scene splits, rough color correction, tracking setup), then refine the results manually. This gives you speed without sacrificing the creative judgment that makes your edit distinctive. Audit AI output before committing.
