# Standardized — AI first draft methodology

## What It Is
The core mindset for AI-assisted editing: AI does the rough first pass, the human keeps final judgment.

## The Rule
**AI is a first draft, never the final decision.**
- AI handles the boring passes: scene splits, rough color correction, tracking setup, subtitles, clean audio.
- The human refines and judges: what stays, what changes, what the final edit feels like.
- Always audit AI output before committing to it.

## Related Ideas (from the wider research)
- **Text-based editing** — transcribe the video, then edit by editing the text; the video follows. This is the Descript-style workflow and the biggest workflow shift in AI editing.
- **Front-load value** — YouTube rewards watch time; tell viewers what they get in the first 30 seconds and use chapters.

## Why It Matters for Leo
- Editing is Leo's weak spot. This mindset means the agent/tool does the first pass and Leo only judges the result — speed without losing quality.
- It is the method behind "the agent advances the editing work" that Leo wants.

## Key Takeaway
Use AI for the first draft of every editing task; keep final judgment. Audit everything AI produces.
