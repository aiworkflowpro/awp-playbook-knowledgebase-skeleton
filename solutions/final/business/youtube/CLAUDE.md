# YouTube

> Main platform — video tutorials on AI agents, built from real hands-on experiments and shared in clear structured steps.

What gets delivered here:

- **Video scripts** — step-by-step walkthroughs of workflows for specific business, work, or industry tasks.
- **Titles and thumbnails** — clear, accurate, keyword-forward titles; thumbnails that match what the video delivers.
- **Descriptions and tags** — searchable summaries and keywords so viewers can find the video.
- **Consistent schedule** — one video per week (adjustable), balancing timely topics with evergreen tutorials that keep bringing viewers in.

Results we watch (from YouTube Analytics): click-through rate on titles and thumbnails, watch time and retention, views, and subscriber growth — all feeding the 10,000-subscriber goal.
