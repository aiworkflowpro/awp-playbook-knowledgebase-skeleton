# Brand Identity

> What AI Workflow Pro is and how it sounds. Voice section is a draft — confirm with Leo.

## What the Brand Is

AI Workflow Pro is Leo's YouTube channel and media brand. It makes industry-focused tutorials on AI agents, sharing hands-on experience from long-term experiments. It is the external-facing side of Leo's one-person business.

## What It Does and For Whom

It teaches people how to use AI agents for real business, work, and industry tasks — turning hands-on practical experience into clear, structured tutorials. The goal is to provide ongoing value through every video.

## How the Brand Sounds (draft)

Based on how Leo works and talks, the brand voice is:

- **Practical** — real hands-on experience first, not theory or hype.
- **Structured** — ideas explained in clear steps, framework-driven.
- **Honest** — shares what was actually tried, including what didn't work.
- **Plain** — the value is in the experience shared, not fancy production.

Example sentence in this voice:

> "Here's the workflow I actually use for this task — the steps I took, what worked, and where it broke."
