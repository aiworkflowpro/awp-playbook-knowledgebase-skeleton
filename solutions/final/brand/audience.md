# Brand Audience

> Who AI Workflow Pro makes content for. Draft — built from Leo's answers; details to confirm.

## Who the Audience Is (draft)

Based on what Leo does and what the brand teaches, the audience is practitioners who want to use AI agents for real work:

- Product managers and builders exploring AI agents for their work.
- People running small or one-person businesses with agents.
- Learners who want hands-on, industry-focused experience — not just theory.

## What Hurts Them (draft)

- They don't know how to turn AI agent ideas into real, working workflows.
- Most tutorials are generic or shallow — no real, long-term experience behind them.
- They lack structured methods for specific business, work, or industry tasks.

## Where They Gather (draft)

- YouTube — searching for AI agent tutorials and practical guides.
- Other channels — to be confirmed with Leo (which social media platforms to publish on).
