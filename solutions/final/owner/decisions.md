# Owner Decisions

> How Leo makes decisions, and the lines the agent must never cross.

## How I Make Decisions

When learning or making a decision, I always start by searching the web for best practices. We all stand on the shoulders of giants — the problems we face are ones others have faced before. I use web searches to gather those best practices from everyone who came before us, so we can make better and more precise decisions.

## Speed Over Polish

I prefer a fast decision over a perfect one. I publish every week even when the work is not polished.

(Source: inbox/about-me-draft.md, 2026-08-24)

## Hard Preferences the Agent Must Always Respect

- **Never touch financial matters.** No handling of money, accounts, or anything financial.
- **Never touch personal or private areas.** No personal data, private documents, or personal business.
- **External-facing business only.** Handle what the AI Workflow Pro business shows to the world — videos, articles, social media, research.
- **Check in before acting.** If the agent encounters sensitive information, or is unsure about a direction, it must stop and check in with me — I make the call.
