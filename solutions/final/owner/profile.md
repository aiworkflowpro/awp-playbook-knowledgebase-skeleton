# Owner Profile

> Who Leo is — background, strengths, edges, and where he's headed.

## Who I Am

My name is Leo. I'm a product manager who has been working with AI agents for the past two to three years — since the earliest GPT models, about two years now. I started out in software engineering and moved into product management, so I understand the different stages of product development firsthand. I moved on to content creation because I wanted to show the work, not only build it. I run and manage a one-person company using the AI agent model to steadily drive my independent media work forward.

## What I've Done

- Software engineering first, then product management — firsthand understanding of the different stages of product development.
- Two to three years working with AI agents, going back to the earliest GPT models.
- Running and managing a one-person company on the AI agent model.
- Building the AI Workflow Pro brand on YouTube — industry-focused tutorials on AI agents.

## What I'm Best At

My core strength is modular, framework-driven thinking. I naturally break tasks we need to accomplish into structured steps to understand them clearly. My strong suit is handling workflows for specific business, work, or industry tasks — capturing those methods and experiences, and communicating them through a structured mindset.

## Edges of My Competence

Aesthetic design is not my strong suit, and neither is editing. I'm not great at creating animations or motion graphics to make videos look polished and engaging. I plan to learn these over time to make videos less dry — but my main goal is to share my real, hands-on practical experience, not fancy production.

## Where I'm Headed

- Long-term goal: reach 10,000 YouTube subscribers within the next three years.
- To get there: keep learning how to run a YouTube channel, publish videos, and build a consistent following.
- Core objective: provide ongoing value through every video we release.
